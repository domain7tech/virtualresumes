How the organizers differ

Same goal — meeting schools face to face — but each organizer runs its fairs differently. Pick based on format, timing, and where you already hold a profile.

Search Associates

10 fairs · all in person

Multi-day weekend fairs where member educators and school leaders interview in the same room — offers can be made and accepted before the weekend ends.

  • All 10 fairs in person, 2–4 days each
  • Strongest January lineup: Melbourne, Bangkok, Hong Kong, London, Cambridge
  • Attendance is for active member educators and schools
Official schedule ↗ · Member login ↗

ISS

9 fairs · 5 in person, 4 virtual

Two formats: in-person EDU Expos, mostly one-day stops across Asia plus Atlanta, and virtual iFair® events you can attend from anywhere.

  • Virtual iFairs in October, December, February, and March — no travel budget needed
  • EDU Expos are single-day events, easier to fit around a school week
  • Build an ISS EDUrecruit® profile before the event; schools review it ahead of time
ISS job fair page ↗

GRC

4 fairs · free for candidates

GRC runs free recruiting fairs for registered candidates. Its 2026–27 dates are Bangkok, Amsterdam, Miami, and Mumbai (via the JPMint Consulting season roundup, updated November 16, 2026).

  • Bangkok Oct 23–24 · Amsterdam Nov 14 · Miami Nov 19–20 (with AMISA) · Mumbai Jan 23
  • Free to attend — register on the GRC portal ahead of time

AMISA

2 events · free

AMISA, the association of American international schools in South America, co-hosts the Miami fair with GRC and runs its own free virtual fair.

  • Miami Nov 19–20 (with GRC) · Virtual Fair Dec 12
  • Info sessions run online in the fall — free, sign up early

Planning around clashes

January is crowded: Search's Hong Kong and London fairs overlap the ISS Shanghai Expo (Jan 15–17), Search's Cambridge fair (Jan 22–25) overlaps GRC's Mumbai fair (Jan 23), and UNI's Waterloo fair (Jan 29–30) lands the same weekend as the ISS Atlanta Expo (Jan 30). You can't do both sides of the Pacific in one weekend — pick a region and commit.

Before you go

ISS publishes this preparation list for its fairs; it applies just as well to Search Associates weekends.

  • Create or update your candidate profile before the event — EDUrecruit® for ISS, your member file for Search.
  • Refresh your resume, credentials, references, and certifications.
  • Review current openings and the participating schools list when it's published.
  • Prepare a short introduction: your experience, strengths, and what you're looking for next.
  • Decide your non-negotiables — regions, curricula, grade levels, school communities — before you're in the room.
  • Follow up with every recruiter you spoke to while the conversation is still fresh.
